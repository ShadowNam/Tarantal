# v1.7d balance: Namibië veld life (D-059)

## What changed in the sim

Every Namibië map gained:
- **an ostrich pair and their nest** on the mid plane (own stream `ostriches`): a bird or the nest
  costs −10 (once per 2 s per pair); their bodies and the nest stop pellets like a prop; a shot
  nearby or the Langloop sends them running;
- **hot-air balloons** (the `balloons` event: 0–1 per hunt, and one more on the calm early-light
  days, `dawn_mist` and `bright_calm`; 1–3 together): a hit costs −25 (once per 2 s per balloon);
  they drift high over the birds' lanes. The `events` stream gains these draws, so the rest of a
  Namibië hunt's event schedule shifts.

The ground, the grass, the oryx and the balloons' look are view-only.

## Gates

- **The brief:** competent scores must not drop by more than about 5 % on any Namibië map.
- **Long-range cap (D-038):** no gun may average over 4 500 competent on a Namibië map.
- Balloons high enough that a normal shot at the birds doesn't hit them by accident.

## Method

- **Before and after on the same build.** "Before" is this branch with the ostriches removed and
  Namibië's events as they were before 1.9.7, by `--set` (`biomes.3.ostriches=[]`,
  `biomes.3.layouts.*.ostriches=[]`, `biomes.3.events=<hadeda, crop sprayer>`). The difference is
  the ostriches and the balloons, nothing else.
- **Command:** `sh tools/namibie-life-sweep.sh <seeds> <out.tsv> ["<maps>"] [bots] [weapon] [sight]`
  (one `npx tsx tools/balance-sim.ts --workers 1` per map and side). Raw rows:
  `docs/balance/v1.7d-namibie-life.tsv`.
- **Seeds:** 500 per cell for the Dubbelloop; 200 for the other five guns.
- **Maps:** the 7 weekday layouts and Saturday's three bonus maps. Competent bot, pointer, Classic.

## Summary

- **Every gun on every map changes by −2.0 % … +2.1 %**, well inside the 5 % allowance (worst:
  the Kettie on Tuesday, −2.0 %; the Dubbelloop's worst −1.7 %).
- **No gun over its cap:** the highest competent mean after is 4 309 (the .22 with the small scope
  on Thursday), under 4 500.
- **Penalties:** +0.0 … +0.7 per hunt (the Dubbelloop on the calm Monday the most: its balloons).

## Accidental hits (what the penalties are)

`npx tsx tools/namibie-life-penalties.ts <layout> 100` (competent, 100 hunts), per hunt:

| Map | Ostrich | Nest | Balloon | Meerkat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | 0.24 (−2.4) | 0.01 (−0.1) | 0.05 (−1.3) | 0.04 (−0.4) |
| Saturday | 0.19 (−1.9) | 0.02 (−0.2) | 0.03 (−0.8) | 0.03 (−0.3) |
| Tuesday | 0.07 (−0.7) | — | 0.01 (−0.3) | 0.06 (−0.6) |

- **Balloons:** 0.01–0.05 hits per hunt in the first lane (`drift.skyY` 70–82). That lane put the
  envelope behind the HUD row (the score box, timer and pause), so the lane moved down to 186–198:
  the envelope's top clears the HUD (≥ 150 design px from the top; `src/sim/balloons.test.ts`), the
  middle stays in the top half of the far birds' sky lane. There it shares the sky with far birds
  and caught stray pellets: 0.23 (Monday) and 0.29 (Sunday) hits per competent hunt. A hit now
  needs **3 pellets of one shot** (`drift.pelletsToHit`; a one-ball rifle still hits with one), so
  a deliberate shot still counts and a stray doesn't. Re-check (competent, 100 hunts, per hunt):

  | Map | Gun | Balloon hits |
  |---|---|---|
  | Monday | Dubbelloop | 0.01 |
  | Sunday | Dubbelloop | 0.00 |
  | Saturday | Dubbelloop | 0.00 |
  | Monday | Plaas-Pomp | 0.00 |
  | Monday | .22, small scope | 0.00 |

  The score tables above are from the first lane; the new lane only lowers balloon penalties.
- **Ostriches:** the pair stands on the mid plane where the mid flocks feed; one pair draws
  0.07–0.24 accidental −10s per hunt. A second pair (tried on the calm days) drew ~1 per hunt
  (0.67–0.80 from the second pair alone, wherever it stood on the pan), so each map has one pair.

## HD texture memory (Pixel 7, Namibië)

Estimated from the packed sheets: ≈ 243 MB of the 256 MB budget (main ≈ 229). The ostriches'
sheet is one 1.5× sheet for Standard and HD (9.2 MB, packed tallest first, frames tight to the
birds), the veld sheet's HD twin 5.0 MB (packed tallest first), the pan-grass props framed to their
content (−1.3 MB). Further cuts would take detail from the grass or the birds; `tests/e2e/perf.spec.ts`
asserts the budget on CI.
