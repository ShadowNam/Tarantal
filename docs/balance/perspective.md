# Perspective: birds get smaller with distance (D-049)

The product owner (26 Oct, playing the Karoo): "the guineafowl, the further away they are, the
smaller they get … the ostriches, because they're far away, are small, but the guineafowl is even
bigger than they are … this needs to apply to all the levels." His Bushveld screenshot showed the
same with zebras.

## What the audit found

`npx tsx tools/perspective-audit.ts` measures what is drawn on each bird plane (tables below,
regenerated by that command). In v1.7 a far guineafowl stood **58 px**, beside springbok drawn
12 px at the shoulder and ostriches 24 px to the head: the bird looked **8–12× too big** for the
animals next to it, and 10–19× for the giraffes and elephants. The far animals had been drawn
for a far plane about 0.03 scale, and the birds at 0.22.

## What changed

- **Plane scales** (`content/scoring.json`): near 1.0 (unchanged; pop-ups), **mid 0.5 → 0.42**,
  **far 0.22 → 0.15**, **Ver 0.1 → 0.07**. Each step back is visibly smaller (263 → 110 → 39 →
  18 px standing). Going smaller than 0.15 on the far plane would make far birds hard to read
  and to hit; the rest of the gap is closed from the other side:
- **Far animals scaled up** (`content/client/feel.json → perspective.faunaScale`): every far herd
  shows at **3×**. Their art is drawn at 3× (D-042), so 3× is the largest size with no upscaling
  until the HD redraw. A far guineafowl now stands below every ostrich and springbok beside it,
  and the remaining oversize is 1.8–4× (the HD redraw draws to the target heights below).
  **D-054 (1.9.1):** done — every far animal (and the Ver waterhole's) is now drawn at its target
  height below (oversize 1.0×), `perspective.faunaScale` is gone, and the per-biome tables'
  fauna rows are re-generated by `npx tsx tools/perspective-audit.ts` (v1.9.0 → now). **Size
  multiplier** (`content/client/feel.json → fauna.sizeMul`): the product owner, 27 Oct 20:10:
  "make the elephants and giraffes at least 25% smaller … the further animals are away, the
  smaller they get … it could even be up to 50%", and 20:15: "make the zebras and wildebeest
  smaller too.", and 22:45, after playing: "It maybe needs to just shrink another 5%, and I saw
  the ostriches didn't change. They are still so big." **Every far species is drawn at 0.57 × the
  target, the ostrich at 0.45** (herds share maps, so one multiplier keeps them in proportion to
  each other; the ostrich alone was still too big; the far guineafowl keep their size): far
  elephant 144 px at the shoulder, giraffe 225 to the head, zebra 61, wildebeest 63, gemsbok 54,
  springbok 36, lechwe 45 at the shoulder, ostrich 89 to the head; the Ver waterhole the same way
  (giraffe 105, zebra 28, gemsbok 25, springbok 17, ostrich 41). One line to tune, then
  `npm run art:interim -- --only fauna` and `npm run assets:pack`.
- **The Ver waterhole** (Namibië): its animals are **re-drawn 5× taller in the same spots**
  (`tools/art/faunaSheets.ts`, `WATERHOLE_HEIGHT_MUL`); scaling the whole sprite would have spread
  them off the water. It sits in its lazy group, never the first download.
- **Props on the mid plane are kept** (windpomp, bakkie, trees, reeds): they are drawn at a
  stage-set scale (a 9 m windpomp would be 2 000 px tall beside a 110 px bird), and many are
  occluders or secrets. Their oversize drops by the mid change (0.5 → 0.42, ×0.84).
- Everything sized "in mid-plane px" (flock spacing, speeds, alert radii, crate drops, dust
  devils) is now written at `scoring.tuningRefScale` (0.5) and scaled by plane scale ÷ 0.5, so it
  shrinks with the birds on every plane.
- **Still shootable**: a body hit radius is at least 18 px on the far plane and 10 px on the Ver
  plane (`planes.*.minHitRadiusPx`, mouse and touch; heads scale as drawn); touch assist far 18 → 12 (mid
  stays 14); far points 25 → 22 (chosen with the 100-seed sweeps below).
- **Size**: bird atlases packed at the new sizes; first download 3 071.5 → 2 965.2 KB gzip (web),
  −106 KB.

## Balance

Cells: `tools/perspective-sweep.ts` (Classic with every huntable gun and three bots, touch
Dubbelloop and .22, the Karoo long-range maps with the shotguns and rifles, the Namibië rifle
cells under the 4 500 cap) and the Season Run (`tools/balance-sim.ts --run`). Before = main at
384bffa (the old scales), run from a worktree; after = this branch. Raw results:
`docs/balance/perspective/`.

### Choosing the hit sizes (100 seeds, same seeds before and after, competent / plinker)

| Try | Classic Dubbelloop (mouse) | Classic Dubbelloop (touch) |
|---|---|---|
| main (before) | 4 169 / 2 444 | 4 327 / 2 433 |
| new scales only | 4 270 / 2 110 | 4 360 / 2 376 |
| + far floor 18 (head floored too), Ver 8 | 4 397 / 2 381 | 4 635 / — (over 4 500) |
| + mid floor 40 | 4 889 / 2 616 (over 4 500) | — |
| body-only floor far 18, far points 25 | 4 323 / 2 191 | 4 503 / 2 312 |
| body floor far 18 / Ver 10, assist mid 10 / far 12, far points 22 | **4 234 / 2 104** | **4 410 / 2 220** |

A bigger hit area helps the competent bot more than the plinker (it shoots more far birds), so
the floor stops at 18 px, and (review) it applies to targets only: don't-shoot birds stay as
drawn, and the Spookjag's bats and lanterns get it too. The plinker loses about 9–14 %: mid birds are 16 % smaller and no floor
applies there (a mid floor pushed competent past 4 500). No gate limits the plinker's loss; the
M3 gate "plinker ≥ 25 % below competent" still passes. The Ver floor 10 px won back half of the
rifles' loss on the Ver plane (Die Verste .22 scope 3 795 → 3 939 at 100 seeds); 12 px added
nothing more.
