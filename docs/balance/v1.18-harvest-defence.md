# v1.18 Die Oes: the raid, the small hole and Phase 2 — balance (D-081)

`npx tsx tools/balance-sim.ts --mode harvest --seeds 500 --bots random,plinker,competent,strong
--workers 2` (and `--input touch`), content as of 1.21.0 (the Dubbelloop, the Harvest's gun).
The bots: `random` sprays; `plinker` and `competent` are the human bots with the Harvest layer
(`harvestHunter` in tools/bots.ts: a warthog or raider in view first, then the birds; in Phase 2
they pan to the most urgent animal — eating, then on its way in — and shoot it, or the ground
beside a piglet, after a human's target-switch delay of 16–26 ticks); `strong` (new, v1.18) is the
competent bot with a steadier aim (6 / 16 px), quicker reactions and follow-ups and a 11–18-tick
switch. "Crop saved" in the first table is the crop when the fence closes (before Phase 2); the
Phase 2 table's "crop kept" is its crop at the end over its crop at the start.

**Targets (the owner, 9 Nov):** Phase 2 unlocks for a competent player about half the time and
for a strong one nearly always; a competent player keeps ~60–70 % of the crop in it, tense.
**Gate:** accidental worker hits < 0.1 a run for the plinker and competent bots.

## Reading

- **The unlock (1,850):** the competent bot gets there 52 % (pointer) / 53 % (touch), the strong
  bot 66 % / 69 %, the plinker 19 % / 24 %. The rush's score depends more on the seed (what comes
  in) than on skill: the strong bot's median rush is only ~200 above the competent bot's, so
  "nearly always" for a strong player would mean an unlock below half the competent rushes. We
  kept the owner's "about half a competent player's".
- **Phase 2 (24 animals, three bursts of 8):** the competent bot keeps 62–63 % of the crop it had
  and stops all 24 (52 % of its hunts end with "All of them!" — every one that unlocked); the
  strong bot 75 %; the plinker 22 % (6–7 % of its Phase 2s end "They ate the lot!"). Mean Phase 2
  bonus: competent ~1,170, strong ~1,270, plinker ~680.
- **The tuning path:** a list of one animal a second (first pass) let every bot stop everything
  before it reached the field (95–99 % kept); bursts of 6 every 6.5 s with drains 3/1.5/3.5 %/s
  swung to 30 %; bursts of 8 with drains 1.4/0.7/1.6 %/s land in the target.
- **The rush:** the competent bot saves 60–61 % of the crop when the fence closes (main 66 %): the
  raid coming down in sight barely changes it; the small hole's two yearlings a rush cost ~5
  points (eating 0.7× an adult's share; at 5 a minute from 3 s they cost ~9). Rush scores rise a
  little (more to shoot: 4.1 warthogs a run, was 2.4).
