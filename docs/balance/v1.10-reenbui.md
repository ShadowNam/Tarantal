**Gate (brief): the competent average must not jump more than ~10 % in the areas where it can
occur.** Every area's average moves +0.5 … +4.5 %; a hunt that has the event scores +7.8 … +10.5 %.

**Tuning.** First pass at `scoreMul` ×1.5: a forced hunt +12 % (Kalahari 3 986 → 4 460,
Okavango 4 245 → 4 775, 100 seeds). Most of the gain is the feeding itself (flocks gathered at
the columns, calmer, two extra flocks): ×1.3 brought a forced hunt to ~+10 % (×1.4: +10.7 / +11.0 %;
×1.5 with one extra flock: +7.7 / +11.8 %). Shipped: ×1.3, two extra flocks.

The Bosveldjagter band (competent mean 2 500 – 4 500) holds for every area's average; a hunt with
the swarm may go over it on the Okavango and the Karoo (4 750), which is the point of the event.
