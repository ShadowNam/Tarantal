# v1.12 balance: Brakkie, the farm dog (D-069)

Content at 1.15.0 (head of PR #112): his feet at y 985, his band round the screen's centre (±450),
pop-ups kept clear of him. Rows: 30 area × gun cells and a can-happy cell.

## What changed in the sim

Brakkie, Oupa's Jack Russell, comes along in Classic, the Daily and the Season Run's plain and omen
hunts (own stream `dog`). His **fetch** is visual only (no points). Balance moves only through his
**bark** (one threat to a mid flock in view, every 14–26 s when one is there) and his **flush** (a
small flock of 3–4 bursting up flushing above him, 20–32 s in and then every 35–55 s, chance 0.7).
A shot **aimed at him** costs −25 (the nest's 2 s cooldown); any other hit only makes him yelp and
bolt. He keeps out of the line of fire on purpose: his spots and the way to them clear of anything
aimed at (340 / 220 px), a pop-up never rises within his room and one already up ducks when he
comes near, he keeps to the bakkie while a wide-spread crate gun is out, and a mid-plane fetch waits
until the spot is clear of live birds.

## Method

- **Before and after on the same build.** "Before" is this branch with Brakkie off
  (`--set brakkie.areas=[]`): he draws only from his own stream, so the difference is him and
  nothing else. (Main's content differs from it only by the Bushveld agama's seat, a tell animal,
  and no replay's events moved for that.)
- **Bot:** `competent` (shoots birds only), Classic, pointer input, each area's Monday map
  (`--layout mon`), seeds 1…n; one cell more with the `canHappy` bot (shoots every tin can) on the
  Karoo's Wednesday, the map whose near fence holds cans at his head's height.
- **Runner:** `tools/brakkie-sweep.ts`, one process at a time, 100-seed chunks (seed offsets 1,
  101, …), merged into `docs/balance/brakkie/final.json`: the Dubbelloop 500 seeds, the other guns
  and the can-happy cell 200.
- **Stray hits** are pellets that struck him without the shot being aimed at him (they cost
  nothing); **aimed** hits are shots whose aim point was on him with no target hit elsewhere (−25):
  for a bot that aims only at birds, both are accidents. The gate counts both.
