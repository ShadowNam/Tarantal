# v1.8b balance: baboon troops and raids (D-062)

Rows: 30/30 area × gun cells. Content at 1.10.0 with the round-4 rule: a mother costs points only from a shot **aimed at her**.

## What changed in the sim

Every Karoo, Bushveld, Okavango and Namibië map (and the Kalahari on about one hunt in three, never
on Friday) has a **baboon troop** on its far ridge (own stream `baboons`): 5–10 members, mothers
on the highest seats. **Raids** (~1.2 a minute): a male comes down to a raid spot on the mid plane;
2 hits bag it for 3 × a guineafowl's base on that plane (an event bonus × Frenzy × Stampede) and
+15 if its raid wasn't done. A done throw or chase threatens the flocks near the spot. Any hit on
the troop, or the Langloop, sends it over the ridge.

**Mothers (round 4, replaces round 2's two-pellet rule):** a mother costs **15** (2 s cooldown)
only when the shot was aimed at her: the fire command's aim point (a rifle's before the sway),
mapped onto the troop's layer, lies inside her body or head ellipse, for every gun. A stray pellet
or stone that strikes her (the Dubbelloop's spread or a Kettie stone's drop at a far bird below the
ridge, the wind) costs nothing and only sends the troop away. `baboons.hit.motherPellets` is gone.
Why: with the two-pellet rule the Kettie (one stone) still drew **0.127** accidental mother hits a
hunt on the Okavango (300 seeds), over the 0.1 gate.

## Method

- **Before and after on the same build.** "Before" is this branch with the map's troop removed
  (`--set biomes.<i>.baboons.troops=[]`): the troops draw only from their own stream, so the
  difference is the troops and their raids, nothing else. The before side doesn't depend on the
  mother rule, so it was not re-run in round 4.
- **Bot:** `competent` (shoots birds only, as in every earlier report); Classic, pointer input,
  each area's Monday map (`--layout mon`), seeds 1…n.
- **Round 4 (after side, every area × gun):** `tmp`-only runner with balance-sim's setup (same
  sim, bot, mode and seeds; checked: Namibië Windbuks before, 200 seeds, gives 2 339 as the
  balance-sim row does). One process at a time, **100 seeds a chunk** (seed offsets 0, 100, …),
  chunks summed: the Dubbelloop 5 chunks (500 seeds), the other guns 3 chunks (300 seeds). It
  counts the mother penalties, every mother hit (penalised or not) and the score. Score changes
  compare the same seeds as the before row (500 for the Dubbelloop, 200 for the other guns);
  mother hits use every chunk.
- **Before rows:** the Dubbelloop 500 seeds and the Karoo / Namibië / Okavango guns 200 seeds from
  `tools/baboon-sweep.sh` (`npx tsx tools/balance-sim.ts --workers 1`); the Bushveld and Kalahari
  guns 200 seeds in two 100-seed chunks of the same runner with the troop removed.
- **Weekdays:** 100 seeds a map for every weekday of every area (Dubbelloop, competent and
  `baboonHappy`, the competent bot that shoots a raider in view first), from round 2 (the
  two-pellet rule). The round-4 rule only takes penalties away, and on the Dubbelloop's Monday it
  moved the mean by under 0.1 %.
- Raw rows: `docs/balance/v1.8b-baboons.tsv` (one block per source file).
