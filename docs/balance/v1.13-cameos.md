# v1.13 Veld cameos — balance (D-075)

The veld cameos (GDD §11.3g) score nothing themselves. They move scores only through the springbok
alarm (one threat to the nearest mid flock), the −10 for a shot aimed at one, and a pellet that
only met a cameo counting as a "thing" hit (not a miss). Gates (the brief): every area within
±3 %, and accidental hits that cost points < 0.1 a hunt for every gun.

**Method.** `tools/cameo-sweep.ts` (one process, 100-seed chunks merged into
`docs/balance/cameos/final.json`; the box runs out of memory with workers). Classic, pointer
input, the competent bot, each area's Monday map, Brakkie on as shipped; *before* is the same build
with `cameos.areas = []` (the cameos' own stream only, so the difference is them), *after* is the
content as shipped. The Dubbelloop 500 seeds, the other guns 100 (seeds 1–100); the can-happy bot
on the Karoo's Wednesday checks the near tortoise beside a tin-can fence. The Kettie cells' *after*
were re-run after review round 1 (its stones now end on a cameo when nothing behind takes them).

**Reading it.** *Stray hits* are pellets that met a cameo with the aim point elsewhere: they cost
nothing and hide nothing (a pellet passes through a cameo to anything behind it), they only play
the reaction; they are many only for the spread guns (Dubbelloop, Plaas-Pomp) on the maps whose mid
herds cross the flocks' band. *Aimed* counts the shots that met a cameo with the aim point on it,
no target hit and nothing aimed at within `hit.guardPx` of the aim point — the hits that cost −10
(fewer, inside the 2 s cooldown): ≤ 0.06 a hunt in every cell (gate < 0.1). The springbok alarm
fires 1.2–1.7 times a Kalahari hunt.
