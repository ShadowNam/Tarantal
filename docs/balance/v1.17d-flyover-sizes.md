## 0. What changes (the proposal, 9 Nov 2026)

Every fly-over bird was drawn and hit at the guineafowl's own units: a Cape turtle dove as long as
a guineafowl. Content `birds.json` `sizeMul` (new, D-083) scales a bird's drawing **and** its hit
shapes (body ellipse and head circle: radii and offsets) on every plane. Measured from the
delivered art (opaque bounds, `tools/sizes.ts MEASURED_ART`):

| bird | role | measure | real | plane | before (v1.20.2) | after (a) | error before → after |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| tortelduif (Cape turtle dove) | target, the Harvest only | bill to tail, gliding | 0.25–0.28 (0.265) m | mid · far | 131.5 · 48.0 px | 57.8 · 21.1 px | +125 % · +130 % → −1.2 % · +1.0 % |
| fisant (Swainson's spurfowl) | target, the Harvest only | bill to tail, gliding | 0.33–0.38 (0.355) m | mid · far | 131.0 · 47.6 px | 77.3 · 28.1 px | +67 % · +70 % → −1.4 % · +0.2 % |
| kwêvoël (grey go-away-bird) | non-target −35 | crest tip to tail tip, perched, crest raised | ~0.525 m | mid | 154.6 px (crest 0.13 m) | 121.0 px (crest 0.07 m) | +33 % → +4.3 % |
| visarend (African fish eagle) | non-target −50, the Okavango | bill to tail, soaring | 0.63–0.75 (0.69) m | far | 62.6 px | 54.4 px | +15 % → −0.0 % |
| visarend | | half its wingspan (raised wing above the body line) | 2.0–2.4 m span (1.05 m) | far | 31.5 px (0.40 m: a 0.9 m span) | 83.5 px | −62 % → +0.8 % |

`sizeMul`: tortelduif 0.44, fisant 0.59, kwêvoël 0.85, visarend 0.87. The kwêvoël's crest was
drawn ~0.13 m (twice a real one's 6–7 cm) and is redrawn at its true length; its field-guide length
(bill to tail tip, flying) was ~0.57 m (+16 %) and is now ~0.51 m (+4 %). The fish eagle's wings were redrawn to a
true ~2.1 m span (they were ~0.9 m).

**Who it touches.** The turtle dove and the spurfowl are the only fly-over **targets** among the
four, and they fly only over Die Oes (the Harvest, `biomes: ["oes"]`): ~2.7 of each cross per
hunt. The kwêvoël (Karoo, Bushveld, Kalahari, Okavango) and the fish eagle (Okavango) are
**penalty** birds: smaller, they are only harder to hit by accident. The far plane's minimum body
hit radius (18 px, D-049) still holds, so a far dove or spurfowl keeps an 18 px body hit circle;
the change bites on the mid plane (a mid dove's body ellipse 32.8 × 20.2 → 14.4 × 8.9 px radii).
Touch aim assist is a fixed px pad on top, unchanged.

## 4. The options

- **(a) True size** (the owner's choice, 1.23.1): art and hit shapes at the real size. The competent bot's
  mean moves −0.2 % … +0.3 % in every Classic area, −0.2 % (pointer) / −2.7 % (touch) in the
  Harvest; nowhere near the 8 % line. The plinker loses the most in the Harvest: −3.4 % (pointer),
  −4.4 % (touch), both inside two standard errors.
- **(b) Compromise:** the art at true size, the hit shapes halfway between (a) and (c)
  (a separate hit multiplier per bird; *not built*, the numbers are from `--set` runs of sizeMul).
  Its numbers sit between (a) and (c) and within noise of both: competent −0.3 … +0.1 % in the
  Classic areas, −1.9 % (pointer) / −2.2 % (touch) in the Harvest; plinker −1.1 % / −0.5 %. The
  hit shapes would then be wider than the drawn bird (a mid dove's 0.72 vs its drawn 0.44: a hit
  ~1.6× its length), which players see as hits in the air.
- **(c) Leave as is:** the dove drawn 2.2× and the spurfowl 1.7× life, the kwêvoël's crest 2×, the
  fish eagle's wings ~0.4×.

No option crosses the ~8 % line for the competent bot, so the compromise is not needed for
balance; it is listed because the owner asked for the choice.
