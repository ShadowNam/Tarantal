## Where the rise comes from

`tools/score-breakdown.ts --bot plinker --seeds 40` (the Karoo), main → branch, points per hunt:

| source | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| Bring! (+15 a delivery) | 28 | 108 |
| Goeie hond! (+30 every 3rd in a row) | 5 | 53 |

He now fetches while the shooting goes on (the owner's "fetch sooner"), so about five more birds a
hunt reach the bakkie and the Bring streak runs longer: about +130 points a hunt, the same for every
skill — +2–9 % for the competent bot, +3–15 % for the plinker. The card itself is within ±1.2 %.
The brief keeps Bring +15 and Goeie hond +30 as they are; with Bring +10 (`--set
brakkie.bring.points=10`) the Bushveld (pointer) still rises 7.5 % (competent 4,494) and 8.9 %
(plinker 2,280), because the streak bonus is the larger part. Left for the owner (D-085): keep it
(a dog that fetches is worth points), or Goeie hond every 5th in a row instead of every 3rd.
