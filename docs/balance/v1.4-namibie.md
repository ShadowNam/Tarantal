# v1.4 balance: the Namibië pack (D-039)

500 seeds per bot, pointer input, Classic mode in Namibië (`--biome namibie`). The per-map table
(the seven weekday layouts and Verder, 48 seeds each) is in `v1.4-namibie-maps.md`. Commands:

```
npx tsx tools/balance-sim.ts --seeds 500 --biome namibie
npx tsx tools/balance-sim.ts --seeds 500 --biome namibie --layout tue
npx tsx tools/balance-sim.ts --seeds 500 --biome namibie --layout tue --weapon langloop --sight scope_var --bots plinker,competent
npx tsx tools/balance-sim.ts --seeds 500 --biome namibie --layout tue --bonus dieVerste --weapon langloop --sight scope_var --bots plinker,competent
npx tsx tools/map-report.ts --biome namibie --seeds 48 --out docs/balance/v1.4-namibie-maps.md
```

Gates: the M3 Classic gates (competent mean in the Bosveldjagter band 2 500–4 500, plinker ≥ 25 %
below competent, plinker above random) on the plain map and a weekday map, for the default gun
and for Die Langloop (the gun the pack is built for); on the bonus maps no gun over 4 500
(v1.3-long-range.md).

## Tuning (before → after)

The first content (every weekday layout at ×1.0 points) gave the Langloop **5 709** on Tuesday,
over the band: the waterhole flock on Ver plus the rifle's Ver ×2 (D-038) made the open pan too
rich for a rifle. Tried and dropped: the Ver flock only from half-time (the Langloop fell to
2 557: a Ver flock that arrives late is barely shot, since every Langloop shot alerts every
flock). Kept: the Ver flock from the start and every weekday layout at **×0.75 points** (dusk
×0.9). Then the Dubbelloop fell from 4 357 to 3 518 on Tuesday, still in band.

- **Quota** (the bonus-map unlock): 3 000. Competent hunts beat it on 69–90 % of the weekday maps
  (Kalahari 88–100 %), plinkers on 13–31 %.
- **Verder:** a mid flock back (the first Verder had none and only 19–35 % of competent hunts beat
  the quota), pop-ups ×0.3, points ×0.95 (on top of the layout's ×0.75). Competent ≥ quota on
  48–81 % of the Verder maps, −8 % to −25 % against the plain map.
- Plinkers do a little better here than in the Kalahari (the near nest tree's pop-ups stay
  close), but the plinker gate holds on every run above.

## What the numbers say

- **Built for long range:** on the weekday maps the scoped Langloop is the best gun (4 370 against
  the Dubbelloop's 3 518), the reverse of the Karoo (2 537 against 4 180, v1.4-langloop.md) —
  which is what the pack is for. Both stay inside the band.
- **The mirage** (12 px on far birds, 5 px on Ver; the hitboxes move with them) is on in the
  Tuesday and Thursday layouts and the bonus maps. Its cost was not measured on its own: the
  mirage maps differ from the calm ones in other ways too.
- **Shotguns still have a hunt:** a mid flock, the far flocks and a few near pop-ups keep the
  Dubbelloop in band even though it cannot reach the waterhole.
