# v1.4 balance: the Namibië pack (D-039)

## Gates

- **Long-range cap (D-038, `v1.3-long-range.md`):** no gun with any of its sights may have a
  competent mean above **4 500** on any Namibië map: the 7 weekday layouts, and the bonus maps
  Verder, Nog Verder and Die Verste. Every player who unlocks Namibië has reached Die Verste, so
  owns a scoped rifle.
- **M3 Classic gates for the default gun (Dubbelloop):**
  - competent mean in the Bosveldjagter band (2 500–4 500);
  - plinker at least 25 % below competent;
  - plinker above random.

## Method

- **Bot:** competent; 200 seeds per cell; pointer input; Classic mode in Namibië.
- **Guns and sights:** every gun with every sight it fits:
  - Dubbelloop, Pomp, Windbuks, Kettie;
  - the .22 with iron, peep and its small scope;
  - the Langloop with its 3–9× variable scope.
- **Maps:** the 7 weekday layouts (Monday to Sunday), plus Saturday's Verder, Nog Verder and Die
  Verste. Saturday is the weekday with the highest points (dusk ×0.9) and is the worst case for
  each bonus map.
- **Command:** `sh tools/namibie-sweep.sh 200 tmp/namibie-sweep.jsonl`, run on the supervisor's
  machine against 1b23897. `python3 tools/namibie-sweep-table.py tmp/namibie-sweep.jsonl` prints
  the table below.

## The fix

The fix is `planePointsMul`, a new map field (D-039, Tech §3.11): per-plane multipliers on a bird's
base points, on top of the map's `pointsMul`. A bonus map's values multiply the layout's.

Only the Ver plane is cut:

| Map | Ver birds' points |
|---|---|
| Monday | ×0.7 |
| Tuesday | ×0.7 |
| Wednesday | ×0.72 |
| Thursday | ×0.65 |
| Friday (also has Ver fly-overs) | ×0.5 |
| Saturday | ×0.50 (was ×0.55; see the margin change) |
| Sunday | ×0.68 |
| Verder, on top of the day's value | ×0.8 |

**Why not `pointsMul`:** a global cut would hit every gun. On Friday, a cut large enough for the
scoped .22 would also push the Dubbelloop (3 274) below the 2 500 floor of the Classic band.

**What stays:** the weekday layouts still score ×0.75 (dusk ×0.9), and the area quota is 3 000.

**Tried and dropped:** a second mid flock with a lower `pointsMul` (Friday: Dubbelloop 2 728,
.22 scope still 4 996).

## What the numbers say

- **Weekday maps:** every cell is under 4 500. The closest are the scoped .22 on Sunday (4 421),
  Tuesday (4 409) and Thursday (4 378); Saturday is 4 337 after the margin change. It is the strongest tool on the open pan, as the
  pack intends, but no longer over the cap.
- **Shotguns:** their Namibië numbers are unchanged (they cannot reach Ver), so the Dubbelloop
  stays in the Classic band.
- **Bonus maps:** all are well under the cap.
  - Saturday's Verder: worst 4 234 (the scoped .22).
  - Nog Verder: worst 3 089.
  - Die Verste: worst 2 324.
  - The shotguns fall to 1 157–1 831 on Nog Verder and Die Verste, as intended: those maps are
    for rifles.
- **Per-map quotas:** the weekday and Verder quota pass rates are in `v1.4-namibie-maps.md`. That
  file comes from the Dubbelloop's map report and is not affected by the Ver change.
