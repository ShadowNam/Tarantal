# v1.7 balance: meerkat colonies on the desert maps (D-044)

## What changed in the sim

Every Kalahari and Namibië map has 1–2 meerkat colonies. They change scores in three ways:
- a shot meerkat costs −10 (at most once per 2 s per colony);
- a meerkat's body stops pellets, like a prop;
- a raptor or the crop sprayer over a colony sets off an alarm that alerts the nearest
  guineafowl flock.

Namibië also gained Oom Faan's crop sprayer (0–1 per hunt), so its sentinels have something to
bark at. The Familiefoto secret (+25, once per hunt) needs 4 s without a shot, which the bots
almost never leave.

## Gates

- **The brief:** competent scores must not drop by more than about 5 % on any desert map.
- **Long-range cap (D-038):** no gun may average over 4 500 competent on a bonus map, and none on
  a Namibië map.
- **M3 Classic gates for the Dubbelloop:**
  - competent in 2 500–4 500;
  - plinker at least 25 % below competent;
  - plinker above random.

## Method

- **Before and after on the same build.** "Before" is this branch with the colonies removed by
  `--set` (`biomes.<i>.colonies=[]`, every layout's `colonies=[]`) and Namibië's original event
  list. So the difference is the colonies and their alarms, nothing else.
- **Command:** `sh tools/meerkat-sweep.sh <biome> <seeds> <out.tsv> ["<maps>"] [bots] [weapon]
  [sight]`, which runs `npx tsx tools/balance-sim.ts` for each map, before and after.
- **Seeds:** 500 per cell for the Dubbelloop; 200 for the rifles and the three-bot gate check.
- **Data:** pointer input, Classic mode. The raw rows are in `docs/balance/v1.7-meerkats.tsv`.
- **Maps:** the 7 weekday layouts, and Saturday's three bonus maps (Saturday is the dusk map
  with the highest points, so the worst case for each bonus map).

Every map changes by between −0.9 % and +4.4 %, well inside the 5 % allowance. Scores mostly
rise a little. Two likely reasons:
- a pellet that stops on a meerkat is not a miss, so it doesn't reset the Frenzy streak (as with
  the weaver nest);
- an alarm sometimes starts a flock running across the view.

The colonies add 0.0–0.2 penalties per hunt for the competent bot.

In the TSV the first Monday rows of each biome are the 500-seed sweep; the later Monday rows
(three bots) are the 200-seed gate run shown at the end.
