# v1.4 balance: Die Langloop (D-038)

500 seeds per bot, pointer input, Classic mode on the Karoo (Monday layout for the bonus maps):
`npx tsx tools/balance-sim.ts --seeds 500 [--layout mon --bonus …] --weapon langloop --sight scope_var`.
The bot raises the scope at its first step (3×); the zoom step changes only what the player sees,
never where a shot lands, so the bot's numbers hold at 6× and 9× too.

**Before** = the first Langloop content (wip 2a37e85): scoreMul near ×0.5 / mid ×0.5 / ver ×2.
**After** = near ×0.65 / mid ×0.65 / ver ×2 (only near and mid changed; Ver stays ×2).
Everything else in the Langloop is as planned: 3 rounds, 1.5 s magazine change, 0.8 s bolt,
windSensitivity 0.5 on the existing wind model, and every shot alerts every flock.

Gates (the task's, and v1.3's for the bonus maps):
- On the plain maps the Langloop scores below both shotguns (Classic competent: Dubbelloop
  4 180, Pomp 4 306), like the .22.
- On the bonus maps no gun's competent mean goes over 4 500 (docs/balance/v1.3-long-range.md).

### What the numbers say

- **Plain maps:** at 2 537 the Langloop sits about 1 650 below the Dubbelloop and 1 750 below the
  Pomp, and below the .22 with every sight (3 620–3 971). The near/mid raise to ×0.65 lifts it
  just into the M3 Bosveldjagter band (2 500–4 500); at ×0.5 it was 2 346. Most of its Classic
  score is secrets and crates, so the bird multiplier moves it only a little (+0.15 ≈ +190).
- **Very loud:** every shot sends every flock running, so the competent bot gets no whole flocks
  and no Twee Vlieë; its edge over the plinker shrinks to 9 % on Classic (the M3 "plinker ≥ 25 %
  below competent" check fails there). That is the gun's design: one careful shot, then the
  whole veld is awake. It is not a gun for plain maps. On the long-range maps the gap is
  48–85 %.
- **Bonus maps:** Nog Verder 4 082 and Die Verste 3 976, under the scoped .22 (4 441 / 4 291)
  and under the 4 500 cap. Ver birds per competent hunt: 14.7 on Nog Verder and 18.3 on Die
  Verste (the .22 scope gets 16.6 and 22.1): fewer shots (3 rounds, a 0.8 s bolt), each worth
  ×2. Verder has no Ver plane, so the Langloop scores low there (2 171), like the shotguns' Ver
  rows.
