# v1.8 Blikkies balance (D-050)

500 seeds per cell, Classic, pointer input, the day's layout (`tools/blikkies-sweep.ts --tag t500`,
raw numbers in `docs/balance/blikkies/t500.json`; 100-seed iteration runs in `t100.json`). Content
at 1.8.0: `birds.json → cans` (kick 430 px/s up, 110 sideways, airHitMul 2.5, pellet cap 1.04,
jitter growth 15, wegChain 10) and `scoring.json → cans` (knock-off 5, air hit 8 + 2 a step up to
the chain cap 8, Weg! 15, × plane near 0.8 / mid 1.3 / far 2 / Ver 2.5; × Stampede only).

Bots: **competent** (GDD §13.8); **can-happy** shoots every can in view first (sees it 250 ms late
and leads it); **apex juggler** knocks every can in view off and shoots it once per arc at the top,
100 ms late, with a 10 px aim error, cooldown and reloads as in play, following it with the camera
(`tools/bots.ts`). "before" = the same map with its fence's cans removed.

Near: a can hit at the top of each arc climbs one fixed step a hit, so a good shot nearly always
takes it out of the top ("Weg!") at chain 4–6. Mid cans are smaller in the air (2.5 × 0.42 of the
near size), so a miss ends about half the juggles first. Far cans are knocked off easily (the
plane's hit floor) but are tiny in the air: far is the hardest. On Ver the bolt-action .22 is too
slow for a second shot before the can lands.
