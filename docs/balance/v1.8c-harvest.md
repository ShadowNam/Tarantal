# v1.8c Die Oes (The Harvest) — balance (D-063)

`npx tsx tools/balance-sim.ts --mode harvest --weapon <gun> --seeds 100 --from <1|101|201|301|401>
--workers 1` (500 seeds per gun and bot, pointer input), content as of 1.11.0 (`modes.json →
harvest`: crop drain guineafowl 0.1, warthog 0.8, baboon 1.2 per second; crop bonus 5 per whole
percent). The plinker and competent bots shoot a warthog or raider in view first in the Harvest
(`harvestHunter` in tools/bots.ts; competent always, plinker half the time), else hunt the birds;
the random bot sprays.

**Targets (plan):** a competent player saves ~70 % of the crop; plinker ~50 %; random ~30 %.
**Gate:** accidental worker hits (shots aimed at a worker that cost the penalty; no bot aims at a
worker on purpose) < 0.1 a run for every gun.

## Reading

- **The Dubbelloop (the default gun):** competent 69.3 % (target ~70 %), plinker 64.2 %, random
  18.8 %; competent scores ~1 300 in 30 s (bag points + a ~340 crop bonus). Tuned from the first
  read (drains × 0.6: competent 81 %, random 49 %) by raising every drain.
- **Worker hits:** 0.000–0.020 a run for the plinker and competent bots on every gun (the gate
  passes; worst: the Langloop's plinker at 0.02). The random bot, which fires at random points
  round the view (the gap is in the middle at the start), draws 0.35–0.89 a run: it isn't aiming
  at a target, so the aimed-at rule charges it — a player spraying at the workers pays.
- **Piglets:** ≤ 0.06 a run for the aiming bots (the Twee-Twee's plinker); a sow's piglets trail
  her, and a shot that hits her is never charged for a piglet.
- **The Kettie and the rifles:** the bots' warthog layer aims straight at a warthog's body; it
  doesn't lead the Kettie's stone drop or raise a rifle's sight, so they bag almost no warthogs
  (0.01–0.05 a run) and save less crop (Kettie 59 %, Langloop 61 %, Twee-Twee 30 %) — a bot
  limitation, not the mode's: a player with those guns shoots the warthogs. The Langloop's loud
  shot flushes the flocks out of the rows, which is why even its random bot saves 39 %.
- **Pomp and Windbuks:** competent 60 % / 59 %: fewer pellets per shot than the Dubbelloop's
  spread on a 2-hit warthog, a fair difference.
