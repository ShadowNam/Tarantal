## Reading (review round 1, 30 Oct)

- **The Dubbelloop (the default gun):** competent 66.1 % (target ~70 %), plinker 63.3 %, random
  2.5 %; competent scores ~1 560 in 30 s. An idle player loses the whole crop: the rush is meant to
  overwhelm anyone who doesn't shoot.
- **Worker hits:** ≤ 0.002 a run for the plinker and competent bots on every gun (the gate
  passes). The random bot draws 0.06–0.12 a run (it sprays round the view and isn't aiming at a
  target, so the aimed-at rule charges it).
- **Piglets:** ≤ 0.016 a run for the aiming bots.
- **The one-pellet guns and the Pomp:** the busier rush puts guineafowl flocks in the rows round
  the warthogs, and a pellet stops at the first bird in front (a bird is tested before a warthog on
  its layer), so the Windbuks (33 %), the Kettie (42 %) and the Pomp (49 %) bag few warthogs; the
  Dubbelloop's spread still reaches them. A player picks the moment (or the bird first); the bots
  shoot straight at the warthog's body.
- **The Kettie and the rifles:** the bots' warthog layer doesn't lead the Kettie's stone drop or
  raise a rifle's sight (the Twee-Twee 8 %, the Langloop 50 % — its loud shot flushes the rows):
  a bot limitation, not the mode's.
- First pass (before round 1): Dubbelloop competent 69.3 %, plinker 64.2 %, random 18.8 %.
