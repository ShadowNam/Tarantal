--workers 1` (500 seeds per gun and bot, pointer input), content as of 1.11.0 (`modes.json →
harvest`: crop drain guineafowl 0.14, warthog 0.9, baboon 1.2 per second; review rounds 1–3: flocks through the gap (up to 12/min, cap 6), peak warthogs 30/min, the redrawn warthogs (175 px) and workers; the bots' Harvest layer tracks a warthog where it is now and takes one with a clear line of fire; crop bonus 5 per whole

## Reading (review round 3, 30 Oct)

- **The Dubbelloop (the default gun):** competent 66.0 % (target ~70 %); competent scores ~1 880.
- **Every other gun's competent bot is at or above ~45 %:** Pomp 52.9 %, Windbuks 52.4 %, Kettie
  54.5 %, Langloop 61.2 %. Round 2's 33 % (Windbuks) and 49 % (Pomp) came from the bots aiming
  where a warthog was 15 ticks earlier: a hit one runs off faster than its (smaller, redrawn) body.
  The bots' Harvest layer now tracks it, as a player does, and picks one with a clear line of fire.
  The game itself is unchanged.
- **The Twee-Twee (10.5 %):** a bot limitation. The rifle bot doesn't raise the sight on a warthog;
  the Kettie's warthog count (0.08) is the same limitation (no lead on the drop), though it saves
  crop by scaring the flocks.
- **Worker hits:** ≤ 0.010 a run for the plinker and competent bots on every gun (the gate < 0.1
  passes). The random bot sprays and draws ~0.1 a run.
- Earlier passes: round 1 Dubbelloop competent 66.1 %, Windbuks 33.0 %, Pomp 48.9 %; first pass
  69.3 % (Dubbelloop).
