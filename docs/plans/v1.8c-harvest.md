# v1.8c Die Oes (The Harvest): a new 30-second mode — plan

Branch `v18c-harvest` from main 1.10.0 (6e3d024). Version 1.11.0. Decision **D-063**.
Product owner, 25 Oct: "a big plantation of crops … everything flocks to it … 30 seconds to shoot
as many animals as possible to keep them out while the fence gets repaired." Decided with them: its
own mode on the title; hit warthogs and baboons go down and are bagged like the guineafowl (no
blood, D-035).

## 1. Rules

- **Mode `harvest`** (`ModeId`, `content/modes.json → harvest`: 30 s, the Classic gun choice, biome
  `oes`, no omen, no layouts/bonus maps, no Stampede, no Daily/Season Run/challenge links).
  Charms: none (Classic has none). Crates: one supply crate (Classic rules, window a fraction of
  the 30 s).
- **Intro, on sim time (~3 s, `harvest.intro.seconds`):** the hunt starts with the camera on the
  gap; a warthog family barges through the fence (posts tip, the wire snaps, the "Oes" sign
  swings), Oupa: "Hulle is deur die draad!". The 30-s clock starts when the intro ends. A tap (any
  fire command) during the intro skips it; the shot is not fired. The skip is a command, so replays
  stay exact. Hunt length in the sim = intro ticks + 30 s (the HUD shows only the 30).
- **The rush (30 s):** spawns come from `content/harvest.json → rush.keyframes` (fractions of the
  30 s, like `pacing`): guineafowl flocks enter at the gap and forage in the rows (the usual flock
  rules: forage → alert → run → flush); fly-ins over the field (francolins and doves; tarentaal
  fly-overs until their sheets land); **warthogs** trot in through the gap (tails up) to a dig spot
  in the rows and dig; **baboon raiders** (v1.8b's sim, sheet and view, reused) come down off the
  koppie behind the farm to `mielie` raid spots in the rows. Rates ramp up over the 30 s.
- **Warthogs (new sim entity, own stream `harvest`):** adult boars and sows are targets: **2 hits**
  bag one (the first sends it running back to the gap, tail up), bagged like a guineafowl (tumble,
  puff, lies still, fades). Points: an event bonus `warthog` = 3 × a guineafowl's base on its plane
  (× Frenzy). A sow may bring 2–3 **piglets**; piglets trail her, never come in alone, and are never
  targets: they cost a penalty (`harvest.piglet.penalty`) only when the shot was aimed at one (the
  fire command's aim point inside its ellipse and not inside an adult's), like the baboon mothers;
  a stray pellet only makes them squeal and run.
- **The crop meter:** starts at 100 %. Every animal eating in the field drains it per second by
  kind (`harvest.crop.drainPerSecond`: guineafowl foraging in a field flock, a digging warthog, a
  raider at its mielie). Bagging an animal or driving it off stops its drain. HUD: a big bar at the
  top, "Crop saved 87 %", flashes (≤ 3 per second, honours "reduce flashes") and a warning sound
  under 40 %.
- **The farm workers (2–3, content):** run to the gap during the intro with posts and wire, then
  hammer and stretch wire there for the 30 s; a "Fence repaired" bar fills with the clock; at the
  end the gap's panel is whole. **Never targets.** A worker costs the big penalty
  (`harvest.workers.penalty`, 100, once per 2 s per worker) only when the shot was **aimed at them**
  (the fire command's aim point inside their body or head ellipse; the baboon mothers' rule, D-062);
  a stray pellet or stone that strikes one makes them duck (~1 s) and Oupa grumble, no penalty.
  **Out of the line of fire:** the workers stand beside the gap, and no animal stand point, entry
  path or flock band lines up with a worker on any plane over the whole camera sweep — a content
  check + unit test like `tools/art/troopLineOfFire.ts`.
- **The end:** at 30 s the fence is fixed, the gap closes, every animal still in runs off (an outro
  of ~1.5 s on sim time, no shooting), then the **Harvest tally**: bagged by kind (guineafowl,
  francolins/doves, warthogs, baboons), crop saved %, best Frenzy, penalties, final score = bag
  points + **crop bonus** (`harvest.crop.bonusPerPercent` × crop saved %), and a local best score
  (`src/meta/storage.ts`). Again / Title / Share (its own share card).
- **Unlock:** after the first hunt (Oupa se Les counts), the same moment the other mode buttons
  appear — a new player meets the Harvest's rush only after learning to shoot.

## 2. Content

- `content/modes.json → harvest` (huntSeconds 30, weapon, biome `oes`, omen false).
- `content/harvest.json` (new, Zod + cross-refs): intro (seconds, sign swing), rush keyframes
  (flocks, warthogs, raids, fly-ins per minute), warthogs (speeds, hits 2, points ×, piglet
  chance/count/penalty, hit ellipses in design px at the reference scale), crop (start, drain per
  kind, warning %, bonus per %), workers (count, spots, penalty, cooldown, duck seconds), outro.
- `content/biomes/oes.json` (new biome used only by the Harvest: no `area`, so it is not in
  Classic's area picker or the Daily rotation; the content check accepts a biome a mode names): layers sky, far_ridge
  (koppie with the troop), far (farmhouse, tractor, windbreak), mid (mielie rows, sunflowers, the
  fence with the gap), near (pumpkin patch and mielie rows), foreground; lanes, feeding spots in the
  rows, `baboons` (troop + `mielie` spots), `harvest` geometry (gap, worker spots, dig spots, entry
  paths). Plane sizes per D-049 (`tuningRatio`), never per-plane factors.

## 3. Sim (`src/sim/harvest/`, pure, lazy like the baboons)

- `harvest.ts` (state, warthogs, piglets, workers, crop, intro/outro), `system.ts`
  (`HarvestSystem` behind a `HarvestHost`, the `BaboonSystem` pattern: extras/plane/shot/step/view),
  registered in `src/sim/lazySystems.ts` (the lazy systems chunk, not the first download).
- Events: `harvestIntroEnded`, `warthogEntered`, `warthogHit`, `warthogBagged`, `pigletScared`,
  `pigletHit`, `workerDucked`, `workerHit`, `cropWarning`, `harvestFenceClosed`. Bonus `warthog`.
- Replays: `harvest` appended to `inputLog.ts` MODES (append-only); the intro skip is the first
  fire command during the intro (already logged), so no new command type.
- Snapshot `harvest`: phase, crop %, repair %, warthogs/piglets/workers (x, y, plane, pose).
- `step()` ≤ 1.5 ms at the peak animal count (unit test like the existing budget test).

## 4. Game (`src/game/`)

- Title: a fourth mode button "Die Oes / The Harvest" (i18n en/af/de/nl, the Afrikaans names
  toggle), a one-line explanation the first time.
- `HuntScene`: the Harvest mode (no weekday layout), waits only for its own area chunk; Classic and
  a resumed Season Run load exactly as before.
- `HarvestView` (lazy): warthog/piglet/worker sprites (one per animal, change-only properties, culled
  off-screen, origin at the feet from the art), the fence panel stages, the sign; hook timer
  `harvestMs`; polls `manifest.upgrade/delivered` like BaboonView. HUD: crop and repair bars.
  The baboons: `BaboonView` as is.
- Tally: a Harvest variant of the Bakkie Tally; share card; local best.
- Admin page: a "Die Oes" entry. Test flags (test builds only): `?harvestHold=<phase>` (hold the
  intro/rush peak/end), `?harvestRush=1` (jump to the peak), `?harvestDuck=1` (a worker ducks),
  `?harvest=0` style flags so other specs never meet the new animals (they only live on `oes`).

## 5. Art and sound (interim, D-035, 2× near; own lazy group `oes` + `oes-hd`, ≤ 1,536 KB gzip)

Field layers (ripe mielies, pumpkins, sunflowers, farm road, farmhouse, tractor — generic, no
brands), the fence (broken, 4 repair stages, the "Oes" sign), warthogs (adult trot/dig/run/tumble,
piglets), workers (2–3 varied people, overalls and hats, run/hammer/duck; respectful, cheerful),
francolin and dove fly-in sheets. Sounds: wire snap, warthog grunts, hammering, crop warning,
harvest jingle. Oupa lines en/af/de voiced (interim TTS), nl text.

## 6. Tests and balance

- Sim: same seed same run; crop maths; worker aimed/stray rule; 2 hits bag a warthog/baboon;
  piglets never targets; the fence closes at 30 s; the intro skip; line of fire; nothing floating
  (every stand point on drawn ground within 2 px); step budget at the peak.
- Golden replay: a Harvest case. Balance (`tools/balance-sim.ts --mode harvest`, 100-seed chunks,
  `--workers 1`): targets — random bot ~30 % crop saved, plinker ~50 %, competent ~70 %;
  accidental worker hits < 0.1 a run for every gun. Report `docs/balance/v1.8c-harvest.md`.
- e2e (CI): start from the title, the intro, a fixed-seed run to the tally, a worker hit costs
  points, Pixel 7 perf at the rush peak (`harvestMs` + frame budget), HD texture memory of the
  `oes` area ≤ 240 MB target (256 budget).
- Screenshot spec `tests/e2e/art-preview-v18c.spec.ts` (ART_PREVIEW=1, ≤ 6 boots per device).
