# v1.18 Die Oes: the raid and Phase 2 — defend the field (D-081, 1.21.0)

The owner's requests of 8 Nov (evening), Phase 2 defaults confirmed 9 Nov.

1. **The raid comes down.** In the Harvest a raid's climb and travel are visible: the raiders
   scramble down the koppie (far_ridge layer, a zig-zag over the rocks: `climb` frames), cross the
   open veld (far_veld layer, run frames), then dash into the rows on the mid plane (run frames,
   `descent.dashMul` × the raiders' run speed). Not targets until they reach the mid plane (they
   are a few px tall on the ridge); on the mid plane the raider rules hold. Content:
   `biomes/oes.json → baboons.descent`.
2. **A small hole in the fence.** `harvest.hole` (oes.json): a low piglet-sized hole left of the
   gap. Young warthogs (yearlings: smaller, targets, 1 hit) squeeze through now and then, sometimes
   with a piglet; the workers patch it at `harvest.hole.patchAt` of the rush. Stand points out of
   the workers' line of fire (harvestLineOfFire.ts).
3. **Phase 2, defend the field.** At the rush's end, score ≥ `harvest.phase2.unlockScore`: a 2-s
   banner, then `phase2.seconds` of a seeded wave list (`phase2.waves`, its own stream
   `harvestPhase2`): warthogs jump the intact fence, piglets squeeze under it, baboons climb over
   the posts, from both ends and over the back fence (mid), round the ends (near). Counter
   "Animals left", a timer, the crop meter. Ends: all stopped, the timer, or crop 0. Bonuses:
   crop × cropBonusPerPercent, perfect, clear.
   New art in a small Standard-only sheet `oes/harvest2` (no HD twin: HD memory).
